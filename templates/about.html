ABOUT

Built by a sound art educator

Hi, I'm {{ site.owner }}. I teach sound art at a university and build kinetic sound sculptures out of Arduinos, servos and stepper motors. Every semester I hit the same problem: the board in the syllabus is sold out, a price changed, or a shop stopped carrying it.

BoardWatch is the tool I wanted. It's one page that tells you which audio-capable dev boards you can buy today, where, and for how much, plus honest specs and a quick picker to help students (and you) choose.

How we check

  • A small script reads each shop's public, structured product data (Shopify product JSON, WooCommerce Store API, schema.org JSON-LD, Reverb's public API) and only falls back to the visible page when there's nothing else.
  • Used listings come from Reverb's public API. Reverb links may be affiliate links.
  • It obeys robots.txt, waits at least 3 seconds between requests to the same shop, and identifies itself with a clear user-agent.
  • Official-store stock is per region. When a shop publishes region-specific availability, the checker reads each store's country. The price and the In stock / Sold out label are that shop's primary store (the US shop for Electro-Smith); a note under the status names the other regions that differ.

  • Specs come only from official maker pages, and each one links to its source. If we couldn't confirm a spec, we leave it blank.
